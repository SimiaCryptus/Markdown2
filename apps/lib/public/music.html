Safety by design

  • Closed language. Sketches are parsed into an AST, never passed to eval or Function.
  • Sandboxed. The player runs in an opaque-origin frame with no access to your page or keys.
  • Zero network. All sounds are synthesized locally; external sample(...) URLs are ignored with a diagnostic.
  • Safety limiter. Audio passes through a non-bypassable limiter, and steady 3–30 Hz pulsing warns before playing.
