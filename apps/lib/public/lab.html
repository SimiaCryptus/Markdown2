1. Resource lab: pack anything

Type any payload, pick a declared media type and an encoding stack. The lab builds the resource with resource(), compresses or encrypts it in your browser, and picks a terminator that cannot collide with your payload. Nothing ever needs escaping.

2. Matryoshka: documents all the way down

A text/markdown resource is a whole document, with its own prose, resources and styles. Drag the slider to nest them up to 16 levels deep. Then tick the box to give every level the same terminator and watch the outermost document close at the first matching line.
